    Methods, provenance & interpretation

    Verified source and historical scope

    Source: National Performance Monitoring and Evaluation Manual, produced by the National Strategy Office, Office of the President, Republic of Botswana. Definitions were visually checked against your local scanned copy on 6 November 2026. The publication date was not verified from the reviewed pages.

    • Table 3.2, p. 23: KPI / CMI / OPI hierarchy and indicator selection.
    • Table 3.4, p. 32: baseline, target, data source, reporting frequency and reporting person.
    • Table 3.6, p. 35: indicative reporting calendar.
    • Table 3.7, p. 37: performance rating colours.
    • Table 3.8, p. 39: standard operational indicators.
    • Table 5.6, p. 99: eight NMES indicators and FY22/23 targets.
    • Table 5.7, p. 100: 12-point ministerial index.

    Printed pages differ from PDF viewer pages by 13. IDs such as NMES-01 and OPI-01 are dashboard identifiers, not official indicator codes. Agency names are retained as printed in this historical source; current responsibilities require confirmation.

    KPI and CMI values must come from the relevant approved national and ministerial performance frameworks. This register does not invent a current national KPI list.

    Corrected traffic lights

    Table 3.7 describes green as target achieved (100%), yellow as 50–100% achieved, and red as less than 50%. To resolve the overlap at exactly 100%, a future higher-is-better implementation will use green ≥100%, yellow ≥50% and <100%, red <50%. This boundary treatment is a dashboard interpretation.

    All ratings are currently “Not assessed”. Historical targets are never silently used as current targets. For time-to-produce reports, vacancy, absenteeism and other lower-is-better measures, an actual/target ratio would be misleading. A direction-specific protocol is needed before automated rating. Budget absorption and revenue measures also need an agreed intended range or direction.

    The manual’s 12-point index is kept separate from indicator attainment. Neither unrelated indicators nor their targets are averaged into an overall performance score.

    Readiness before data collection

    Each indicator needs an approved operational definition, unit, baseline, target and period, data source, reporting frequency, accountable officer and reporting unit. Corrective actions need an owner and deadline (pp. 36–37). Current values remain blank until verified.

    Download: Source-linked definitions and index questions (JSON). CSV export includes the current register selection. The manual PDF itself has not been uploaded.

    Data handling: public source definitions only; no personal records, credentials, analytics, data upload or AI calls. The reference basemap uses Leaflet and © OpenStreetMap contributors. Library and tile requests go to their external providers.
